## Supplier-specific control boundary

The customer must provide the supplier or investee name, methodology and version, reporting period, lifecycle boundary, assurance status and evidence reference. The platform preserves the reported result and allocation calculation without inventing a generic UK Government factor. Results that are not third-party verified retain a calculation warning and cannot pass the automated assurance-readiness gate until independently reviewed.

This route does not claim that the supplier result is independently verified by DcarbN Analytics. External assurance remains a separate requirement.
