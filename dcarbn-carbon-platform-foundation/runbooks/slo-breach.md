# SLO Breach

1. Identify the breached objective and current burn rate.
2. Freeze risky releases when the fast-burn or exhausted-budget alert is active.
3. Use traces and logs to isolate the failing routes and dependencies.
4. Reduce load, disable nonessential workflows, or roll back the triggering release.
5. Continue incident updates until the SLI is stable and budget consumption is understood.
6. Record whether the SLO target, implementation, or capacity plan requires revision.
