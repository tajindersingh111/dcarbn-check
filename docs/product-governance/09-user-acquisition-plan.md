# First 100 users acquisition plan

## Definition

Count an “activated user” only when they join a tenant and complete a meaningful workflow step. The initial target is approximately 20–30 organisations producing 100 activated users, not 100 unqualified registrations.

## Ideal early customers

UK final-mile logistics operators, fleet-owning SMEs, 3PLs and reporting teams that currently combine spreadsheets, fuel/fleet data and external advice. Prioritise an urgent reporting need, accessible decision-maker, usable data and willingness to provide structured feedback.

## Acquisition sequence

### Founding design partners (first 10–20 users)
Recruit through Leonard’s network, D-carbN relationships and trusted partners. Offer guided onboarding and a defined pilot in exchange for feedback, evidence and—only with permission—a reference.

### Direct account outreach (next 30–40)
Build a focused list of 50–100 target organisations. Use a five-touch sequence based on reporting risk, fragmented operational data, audit lineage and a short demonstration. The call to action is a readiness assessment or pilot, not a generic free trial.

### Partnerships and communities (next 20–30)
Engage logistics associations, fleet consultants, accountants, sustainability advisers, universities and technology/data partners. Provide a governed reporting checklist, webinar or anonymised demonstration.

### Content and events (remaining users)
Publish practical material: Scope 3 category checklist, fleet/freight methodology explainer, evidence-readiness scorecard, comparison examples and reporting-control guidance. Use LinkedIn, targeted email, webinars and selected logistics exhibitions.

## Funnel

Target account → engaged contact → discovery → qualified demonstration → pilot → activated tenant → approved report → paid conversion/reference.

Track contacts and organisations separately. Weekly measures: response, meeting, demonstration, pilot acceptance, activation, time to first inventory, report completion, paid conversion and referral.

## 90-day operating rhythm

- Weeks 1–2: proposition, list, scripts, pilot terms and case-study consent.
- Weeks 3–6: founder-led outreach, demonstrations and two design partners.
- Weeks 7–10: partner webinar/content, pilot evidence and objection learning.
- Weeks 11–13: conversion offers, references and repeatable onboarding.

## Guardrails

No bought mass lists without lawful basis and quality review. Use UK GDPR/PECR-compliant outreach, clear opt-out and minimal data. Never publish customer emissions or names without written permission. Acquisition promises must match MVP scope.
