# Growth plan

## North-star outcome

Number of customer organisations completing an approved, evidence-backed inventory/report in the platform. Registrations, imports or page views are supporting signals, not the product outcome.

## Growth loops

1. **Reporting loop:** completed report creates renewal need for the next period.
2. **Collaboration loop:** contributors, reviewers, approvers and auditors are invited into the same tenant.
3. **Integration loop:** successful mappings reduce future reporting effort and increase retention.
4. **Partner loop:** accountants, consultants and logistics partners onboard repeat customers.
5. **Evidence loop:** aggregated, permission-safe learning improves templates and guidance without exposing customer data.

## Phases

### Pilot to product-market evidence
Prove activation, accurate governed outputs, manageable support and willingness to pay. Ship only changes repeated across customers or required for integrity/security.

### Repeatable UK logistics motion
Standardise onboarding, CSV/accounting mappings, demonstrations, pricing, support and case studies. Establish funnel and cohort reporting.

### Expansion within customers
Add entities, users, reporting periods, integrations and premium support. Expansion follows verified use, not forced complexity.

### Adjacent verticals and geographies
Enter only after assessing factors/methodologies, regulations, currency/tax, privacy, localisation, support and partner coverage. UK logistics remains the reference segment until the motion is repeatable.

## Metrics

Activation: setup completion, first import, first calculation, first approval/report and time to value.  
Quality: rejection rate, missing evidence, calculation warnings and restatements.  
Engagement: active contributors/reviewers, repeat periods and connector use.  
Commercial: conversion, ACV, CAC/payback, gross margin, renewal, expansion and NRR.  
Service: tickets per tenant, resolution time, uptime, restore/failover evidence and customer satisfaction.

## Experiment governance

Every experiment states hypothesis, segment, primary metric, guardrail, duration and decision rule. Do not run experiments that weaken calculation governance, security, consent, accessibility or audit history. Feature flags require ownership and expiry.

## Product priorities after launch

Rank with evidence: reduce time to first inventory; improve import mapping/error correction; activate live provider integrations; simplify evidence collection; improve report collaboration; add entitlement/billing controls if packaging requires them; and create partner administration only after repeatable demand.

## Review cadence

Weekly during pilots, monthly through first 100 users and quarterly thereafter. The Product Owner maintains the growth backlog; Commercial and Technical Owners jointly approve experiments affecting price, data, integrations or production risk.
