# MVP scope

## Version-one objective

Enable a UK organisation to create a governed Scope 1–3 inventory from manual, CSV and approved operational/accounting sources; review evidence and exceptions; approve and lock the inventory; and export an audit-ready report.

## In scope for launch

- Secure multi-tenant accounts, invitations, roles, MFA and recovery.
- Organisations, entities, sites, reporting periods and boundaries.
- Base-year governance, materiality and restatement history.
- Manual activity entry and governed CSV import.
- DATa vehicle/freight integration and comparison workflows.
- Governed methods for Scope 1, Scope 2 and all 15 Scope 3 categories.
- UK Government factor registry, normalisation and resolution lineage.
- Scope 3 screening, data review, approvals and immutable inventory locking.
- PDF/CSV reporting and audit lineage.
- Connected-systems workspace, connection records and sync reconciliation.
- Operational monitoring, backup/recovery and supply-chain controls.
- User guide, operating manual, privacy/security and support procedures.

## Launch dependencies not yet equivalent to “done”

- Paul’s walkthrough and written business acceptance.
- One controlled pilot customer and completed acceptance evidence.
- Production hosting/network/domain/SSL/secret-manager decision.
- Vendor developer accounts, OAuth apps and callbacks for live connectors.
- Independent penetration test and remediation.
- Managed identity-provider decision or formal self-hosted risk acceptance.
- Legal terms, privacy notice, data-processing terms and retention schedule.
- Production support ownership and incident contacts.
- Commercial packaging and contracts.

## Explicitly out of scope for version one

- Automated assurance/certification by D-carbN.
- Unreviewed AI classification or automatic inventory approval.
- Arbitrary country factor libraries beyond approved governed sets.
- Native mobile apps or offline operation.
- Public marketplace, advertising model or customer-to-customer data sharing.
- Self-service card billing unless separately approved.
- Custom connector development for every customer.
- Real-time telematics promises where the source contract is batch based.
- Predictive decarbonisation optimisation presented as verified accounting.
- Unlimited bespoke report design.

## Scope-control rule

A proposed launch addition must address a legal, security, data-integrity or pilot-blocking need. Otherwise it enters the post-launch backlog with evidence of demand. “Useful” is not the same as “launch critical.”
