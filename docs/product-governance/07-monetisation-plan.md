# Monetisation plan

## Recommended model

Use B2B SaaS subscriptions with implementation/onboarding and optional professional services. Do not use advertising: it conflicts with audit trust and customer-data sensitivity. Do not launch a marketplace until there is governed partner demand.

## Packaging hypothesis for validation

| Package | Intended customer | Included boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | SME starting governed reporting | One tenant, limited users/entities, manual/CSV, core reporting |
| Professional | Growing logistics/operator customer | More users/entities, DATa integration, full Scope 3 workflows, approval/audit tools |
| Enterprise | Multi-entity or assurance-heavy customer | SSO/managed identity options, advanced integrations, support SLA, recovery/security evidence |

Possible paid additions: onboarding/data mapping, bespoke connector, historic-data migration, additional entities or usage, supplier-engagement support, assurance preparation, branded reporting and premium support.

## Commercial principles

- Price for governed reporting value and operational complexity, not raw database rows alone.
- Use annual agreements where possible; invoice-led billing is acceptable for launch.
- Define fair-use/volume bands for imports, connector synchronisations and storage.
- Separate product subscription from one-off implementation.
- Do not promise certification, legal compliance or emissions accuracy beyond the evidence and methodology used.
- Discounts require term, reference/pilot value or reduced scope; avoid permanent unstructured discounts.

## Unit economics to track

Annual contract value, implementation revenue, gross margin, cloud cost per tenant, integration/support hours, sales cycle, acquisition cost, payback period, renewal, expansion and gross/net revenue retention.

## Validation actions before final pricing

Interview 10–15 target customers and test three packages. Compare willingness to pay against current spreadsheet/consultant cost, reporting risk and time saved. Pilot terms must state the post-pilot price or pricing decision date. Record which features actually affect purchase.

## Billing implementation decision

Launch recommendation: contract plus invoice, with subscription entitlements administered by D-carbN. Add self-service billing only when transaction volume justifies it. Any payment integration uses hosted checkout and verified webhooks; D-carbN stores customer/subscription references and entitlements, never card details.

## Decision owner and gate

The Commercial Owner approves price, contract term, included usage, onboarding fee, support SLA and pilot conversion offer before production launch. Product must expose entitlements before multiple commercial packages require technical enforcement.
