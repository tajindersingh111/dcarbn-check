Demonstration checklist

Facilitator: Leonard

Run a controlled, repeatable New Era demonstration.

Complete the checks in order. Use the committed fictional samples or irreversibly anonymised 2025 data only.

Before the customer session

  • Confirm the working branch is agent/new-era-browser-pilot.
  • From the repository root, start the application with python3 -m http.server 8081 --directory local-demo.
  • Open http://127.0.0.1:8081/ on Leonard’s demonstration laptop and confirm there are no browser-console errors.
  • Open Inventory wizard → Clear all local customer data, read the warning, confirm the clear, and reload the page.
  • Confirm the visible reporting-period default is Calendar year 2025.
  • Create the agreed demonstration passphrase. Do not write it in this checklist, email it or display it in screenshots.
  • Prepare the committed fictional organisation, Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 sample CSV files. Do not use real New Era records.
  • Close unrelated applications, notifications and documents before screen sharing.

Demonstrate the customer journey

  1. Explain the local data boundary

    State that files, calculations and session state stay inside this browser on Leonard’s laptop. Independent remote access is not part of this pilot.

  2. Load the fictional organisation metadata

    Upload local-demo/sample/fictional-organisation-2025.csv. Show the organisation name, reporting boundary and Calendar year 2025 dates.

  3. Show a blocked error and its correction

    Create a temporary copy of a fictional sample outside the repository. Introduce an impossible date or unsupported unit, upload it, and show the row-specific correction guidance and blocked readiness. Correct the temporary copy and re-upload it.

  4. Show duplicate detection

    In another temporary copy, repeat a source record ID. Show that the duplicate blocks submission. Remove the repeated row or apply a truthful unique ID, then re-upload. Delete all temporary files after the demonstration.

  5. Load all three scopes independently

    Upload fictional-scope-1-2025.csv, fictional-scope-2-2025.csv and fictional-scope-3-2025.csv through their matching controls. Show that each receives its own validation summary.

  6. Complete and submit

    Complete the contributor confirmations, review the readiness panel and select Submit to D-carbN. Emphasise that blockers prevent readiness from reaching 100%.

  7. Switch to the D-carbN reviewer role

    Use the role control to switch from contributor to analyst/reviewer. Inspect totals, the versioned 2025 factor pack, evidence references and audit history. Approve all ready records and complete the analyst review.

  8. Approve and lock the report

    Switch to the authorised approver role. Resolve final controls, generate the immutable snapshot, inspect the report, and lock the release. Confirm that calculations and lineage remain visible after locking.

  9. Create an encrypted session backup

    Select Export pilot session, provide the approved passphrase and save the encrypted export. Demonstrate restoration only if agreed in the session plan. Never share the export without its protected transfer procedure.

Close the session safely

  • Confirm any required report and calculation exports were captured and contain fictional or irreversibly anonymised information only.
  • Use Clear all local customer data and confirm the application returns to a clean Calendar year 2025 state.
  • Delete temporary error/duplicate CSV files and remove unnecessary downloaded session or report files from the laptop and bin/trash.
  • Close the browser tab and stop the local server with Control-C in the terminal.
  • Record feedback in the UAT form without names, contact details or sensitive business data.
Stop condition. If real, identifiable, confidential or commercially sensitive information is displayed or selected, stop the demonstration, do not upload it, clear any local copy created by the pilot, and record only a non-sensitive description of the incident.
